Frequently asked questions

What share of Chinese indie developer products are still maintained?

Across the whole database, roughly 85.1% of products are still live and 13.7% are no longer maintained. The figure moves with age: products first listed in the current year are almost all still active, while older cohorts have naturally lost more of their number.

Why do older products stop being maintained?

The same reasons anywhere: the developer moved on, the project merged into something else, the market didn't materialise, or it was a learning project that served its purpose. A non-maintained product isn't a failure — many were deliberately finished rather than abandoned.

Where does this data come from?

The open-source repository chinese-independent-developer, which Chinese indie developers submit their work to. Our directory syncs it daily. We count only what appears there — we don't scrape stores or estimate market size.

Can I reuse this report or its numbers?

Yes, with attribution. Citing 'AI 独立制造所 (indiemaker.cn) — Chinese indie developer survival report' and linking back is enough. The underlying data is open source.

How current is this data?

The dataset is re-synced daily, and the counts on this page are regenerated on every build, so they reflect the latest sync rather than a frozen snapshot.
