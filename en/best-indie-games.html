Frequently asked questions

Do I need to download or install these games?

No. They run in a browser tab — open the page and play. A few also ship as desktop builds, but the browser version works first.

Are they really free?

Yes, these are free to play. Some are open source. There's no purchase step on our side — this directory never charges anything.

What kinds of games are here?

Casual and idle games, brain and focus training (memory, attention, Schulte tables), party and multiplayer games, music creation toys, sandbox and role-playing experiments, and utilities that help with games.

Can I play them on a phone?

Most work on mobile browsers, and many are designed mobile-first. A few that need a keyboard are desktop-only — check the product page.

My studio made a game — how do I get listed?

Submit a pull request to the upstream open-source repository, chinese-independent-developer. The directory syncs from it every day.
