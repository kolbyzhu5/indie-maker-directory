Online tools that never upload your files

Curated 190 browser-local tools · updated 2026-10-05

Selection criteria: the tool states that it processes files locally or collects no data; it works without an account; and it solves a real task. Product descriptions are machine-translated from the developers' original Chinese (the original text stays untouched on every product's Chinese page). Every listing links to our English product page, which in turn links to the product's official site.

Frequently asked questions

How can I tell whether an online tool uploads my file?

Three checks that actually work. First, read the description: a genuinely local tool says so plainly ('runs locally', 'file never leaves your device', 'no upload'). Second, load the page, disconnect from the internet, and use it — if it still works, the processing is local. Third, open your browser's Network panel and watch while you process a file: a local tool produces no large outbound request.

What kinds of tools work without uploading files?

Image processing (background removal, watermark removal, resizing, compression), PDF handling (compare, merge, convert), audio and video conversion, and a large set of small utilities. Modern browsers can do far more locally than most people assume.

Is browser-local processing as capable as a server?

For files up to a few hundred megabytes it's usually indistinguishable and often faster, since there's no upload and no queue. The constraint is your device's memory, not the network. Very large jobs are still better served by a desktop application.

Can I use these for ID photos, contracts or medical records?

That's exactly the use case this page is built for. Prefer tools that state they process locally and collect no data, and verify with the Network-panel check above. No upload means no third party can retain a copy.

Do these tools work offline?

Once the page has loaded, many do — which is also the simplest way to verify the claim. Those that need a first network request for the app itself will still usually keep your file local afterwards.
