Frequently asked questions

Are there free online PDF tools that don't upload my file?

Yes, and this page favours them. Tools listed under browser-based processing state that the file stays on your device — useful for contracts, invoices and anything confidential. You can verify by opening your browser's Network panel while processing a file: no large outbound request means it's local.

How do I convert Excel or images to PDF?

Several tools on this page do exactly that. One converts spreadsheet data to PDF with a page-preview before export and can merge multiple workbooks; others handle images. All work in the browser without installing Excel or Acrobat.

Can I batch-generate certificates or payslips?

Yes. One tool comes with ready-made templates for certificates, contracts, payslips and invoices — import an Excel or CSV list, map the fields, and it generates a personalised PDF per recipient. Another focuses purely on certificates at scale.

How do I compare two versions of a PDF?

Use the PDF comparison tools listed here. They highlight what changed between two documents, which is far faster than reading both side by side — common for contracts and versioned specs.

Can I turn a scanned PDF back into editable text?

Yes, with the OCR tools on this page. They recognise text in scanned documents and export to an editable format such as Word or plain text. Accuracy depends on scan quality.
