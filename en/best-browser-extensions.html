Frequently asked questions

Which browsers do these extensions support?

Mostly Chrome and Edge, and many also work in Firefox or other Chromium browsers. The individual product page says which stores it's published in.

Are these extensions safe to install?

We list the developer's own description without rewriting it, and every entry links to the product's official page and store listing. Extensions request browser permissions — check those in the store listing before installing, as you would with any extension.

What can a new-tab or bookmark extension actually do?

Typically: replace the new-tab page with something useful, manage large numbers of bookmarks and tabs, switch between search engines, and sync settings without an account.

Are there extensions that keep data local?

Yes — several explicitly state they collect no personal data and store settings locally. Those are marked in the product description.

Do I have to pay for these?

Most are free. A few have a paid tier for extra features, which is the product's own model. The directory itself is free.
