Frequently asked questions

What counts as an AI productivity tool here?

Anything that helps you manage attention, information or output: usage tracking for AI subscriptions, focus timers and habit trackers, note-taking and knowledge bases, translation and social publishing, and learning tools.

Can these tools manage my AI subscription usage?

Yes — a few track spend and quota across AI providers, which is genuinely useful if you pay for more than one model. They surface where your budget is actually going.

Do I need an account?

Some work entirely locally with no account. Others sync across devices, which requires one. Each product page says which.

Are note-taking tools here cloud or local?

Both kinds exist. Local-first note tools store everything on your own machine; cloud ones sync across devices. We list both, and the product description indicates which.

Are these tools free?

Many are free or open source, some use a free-plus-paid model. This directory is free, with no membership and no paid ranking.
